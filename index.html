Where are the Flock cameras?

Newport News has Flock license-plate cameras. Tap the map to place guesses where you think they are. Tap a guess again to remove it.

Your score, in miles, combines two things: how close your guesses land to real cameras (accuracy) and how well your guesses cover all the cameras (coverage). Lowest score wins.
